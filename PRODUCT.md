# Product

**User.** Teachers, students, journalists and librarians answering "how democratic is X, and how did it change?" with a
source they can cite; plus anyone who wants to reuse the numbers.

**Painful job.** V-Dem's raw data is hard for non-experts, the EIU index is paywalled, and charts can't be dropped into a
lesson or article with correct attribution.

**Promise (this companion).** A chart of any country's score since 1900 that you can paste anywhere, already attributed
under CC BY-SA; the data as plain files; and public proof that the numbers rebuild from V-Dem's own file.

**Wedge.** Embeds and reproducibility. democracys.org owns the reading experience and country-score searches; this
companion owns "democracy chart embed", "democracy score data download" and the trust question.

**Why now.** Democratic backsliding is a constant classroom and news topic, and V-Dem's next release (March 2027) will
arrive here automatically.

**Activation metric.** An embed placed on another site (referrers to `/embed/<country>/`, counted by GoatCounter once
enabled) or a data download.

**First revenue event.** None on GitHub Pages. Slot: `SUPPORT_URL` (grant or classroom sponsor link).

**Won't do.** Compete with democracys.org for search traffic on country pages, add causal country narratives, or rank
local institutions.
